# About me (used to write recruiter emails)

Fill this in once and save it as about_me.md (that file stays private: it is in .gitignore).
The email writer only uses what is written here and in your resume, so anything you leave
out will never be claimed. Keep it short and true.

- Name: Your Name
- Signature:
    Your Name
    555 555 5555 | you@example.com
    linkedin.com/in/you | github.com/you
- Looking for: the roles and places you want, e.g. Software Developer or Full Stack roles in Toronto or remote in Canada
- Work authorization: e.g. Canadian citizen; would need sponsorship for US-based roles
- One-line pitch: who you are in one sentence

## Proof points

Real achievements the writer can pick from. Use numbers only where you have them.

- Company, role: what you built or fixed, how, and the result
- ...

## Anything to avoid

- Topics or claims you never want in an email

## Tone

- Direct, friendly and short. No em dashes.
