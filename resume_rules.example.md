# Resume rules (example)

Copy this file to `resume_rules.md` and make it yours. When you press **Tailor my resume**, Claude
follows these rules to rewrite your base LaTeX resume (`resume.tex`) for one posting.
`resume_rules.md` is personal, so it stays out of git.

## How to tailor

- Mark the posting's hard requirements MET, PARTIAL or MISSING from the resume alone.
- Headline: 4 to 6 terms, each backed by a bullet below. No claim without proof.
- Put the most relevant bullets first within each role.
- Bullet formula: strong verb, what you built, how, and the outcome or scale.
- Prefer outcomes (speed, adoption, users, time saved, errors reduced) over feature lists.
- Only use numbers already in the resume. If a bullet would be stronger with a number you haven't
  given, write it without one and list it under "numbers to find".
- Reframe adjacent experience honestly (for example, another headless CMS for a Sanity role), but
  never add a tool, language, title, date or domain you haven't used.
- Skills: the posting's tools you know first; drop unrelated ones to save space.

## Always

- One page.
- No summary section.
- No em dashes.
- Keep the LaTeX template, its commands and packages as they are.

## Things only you know (examples, replace with your own)

- Write my current employer's name exactly as "Acme", with nothing after it.
- Keep the publication under my master's degree.
- Don't mention relocation in the headline.
